## Design Context

### Users
Developers attending tech conferences, hackathons and community meetups in Spain. They use phones at events and desktop between events to discover encounters and maintain their accreditation. Context comes from PRODUCT.md and the requested standalone event explorer.

### Brand Personality
Quiet, physical and precise. Short Spanish copy, like fields printed on an event badge.

### Aesthetic Direction
Follow DESIGN.md: warm paper and ink, Space Grotesk with JetBrains Mono for printed labels, hairline dividers, pill controls and a small orange signal. Flat event lists and maps; the accreditation is the lifted object. No gradients or neon.

### Design Principles
1. Keep the accreditation as the personal entry point; offer a dedicated public page for exploring the event catalog at full scale.
2. Preserve the same city and date filters across map and list views.
3. Keep online events and events without map coordinates discoverable in the list.
4. Adapt to phones, retain keyboard access and respect reduced motion.
5. Use the existing catalog and label examples; do not invent attendance, events or dates.
